Sri Kumaran Hospital is located in Manapparai, Tamil Nadu.

Sri Kumaran Hospital provides healthcare services to patients
through multiple medical departments.

The departments available at Sri Kumaran Hospital include
Orthopedics, General Medicine, Cardiology, Pediatrics and Neurology.

The hospital provides diagnostic services including Spiral CT Scan,
Ultrasound, Doppler, ECHO, ECG and X-Ray.

The hospital also provides a 24-hour laboratory, pharmacy
and blood bank facilities.
